About the Journal (UoK - JISD)

Manuscript Submission Guidelines

  • Language & Originality: Manuscripts must be written in English, original, and not under consideration elsewhere[cite: 1].
  • File Format: Only Microsoft Word format (`.doc`, `.docx`) is accepted[cite: 1].
  • Free Formatting: Initial submissions may be made in free format with minimal formatting requirements[cite: 1].
  • Peer Review Model: All submissions undergo a rigorous double-blind peer review process[cite: 1]. Ensure no author names or institutional affiliations appear within the file body[cite: 1].
