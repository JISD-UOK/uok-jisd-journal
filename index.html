About the Journal

The Journal of Integrative Science Development (JISD) is a peer-reviewed, open-access academic journal published by the University of Kabridahar. JISD serves as a multi-disciplinary platform dedicated to bridging scientific research, technology transfer, and socio-economic development.

Author Guidelines & Editorial Policy

  • Peer Review Process: All manuscripts undergo strict double-blind peer review.
  • File Format: Microsoft Word (`.doc`, `.docx`) only.
  • Anonymity: Ensure author names, emails, and affiliations are removed from the main manuscript text file.
  • Publication Fees: No article processing charges (APCs) for inaugural volumes.
