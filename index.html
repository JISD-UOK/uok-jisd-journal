About the Journal

The Journal of Integrative Science Development (JISD) is a multi-disciplinary academic platform hosted by the University of Kabridahar. We publish original research, field studies, and critical reviews across diverse scientific domains.

Author Guidelines & Peer-Review Policy

  • Peer Review Process: All submitted manuscripts undergo double-blind peer review.
  • File Format: Microsoft Word (`.doc`, `.docx`) format required.
  • Anonymity: Ensure author names and institution details are removed from the submitted manuscript file.
  • Editorial Contact: Official queries should be sent to jisd.manuscript@uok.edu.et.
