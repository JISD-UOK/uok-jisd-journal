About the Journal (UoK - JISD)

UoK JISD serves as a dynamic forum for intellectual exchange on issues affecting humanity and transformative interventions. We bridge the gap between science, technology, and society through high-quality interdisciplinary research and meaningful knowledge transfer.

Manuscript Submission Guidelines

  • Language & Originality: Manuscripts must be written in English, original, and not under consideration elsewhere.
  • File Format: Only Microsoft Word format (`.doc`, `.docx`) is accepted.
  • Free Formatting: Initial submissions may be made in free format with minimal formatting requirements.
  • Peer Review Model: All submissions undergo a double-blind peer review process. Ensure no author names or institutional affiliations appear within the file body.
